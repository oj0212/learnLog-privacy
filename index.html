LearnLog — Privacy Policy

Chrome extension used in an academic research study
Last updated: [DATE]

LearnLog is a research tool operated by [INSTITUTION NAME] for a study of how students learn with AI assistants. It is used only by participants enrolled in that study. This policy explains exactly what the extension collects, why, and what control you have over it.

Nothing is collected until you agree, three times over. You must sign in with credentials issued by the study team, tick the study consent box, and then answer a prompt for each individual conversation asking whether that chat should be logged. A conversation you decline is never recorded.

2. What the extension collects

Only on four websites

The extension runs only on chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.ai and gemini.google.com. It cannot see any other browser tab, your browsing history, your files, or any other application on your device.

Account information

  • The email address used to create your study account, and an authentication token that keeps you signed in.
  • Your study cohort identifier.
  • Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only as one-way cryptographic hashes. Nobody, including the research team, can read your password.

Conversations you choose to log

For a conversation you have agreed to log, we record:

  • The messages you send and the AI assistant's replies.
  • The topic and learning context you selected for that chat.
  • Interaction timing: how long you spent reading a reply, how long you spent composing a message, edits and pastes while composing, and whether you left the tab.
  • Basic events such as copying text from a reply, regenerating a reply, and returning to earlier messages.
  • The time of the interaction, and your timezone offset so that time-of-day can be interpreted correctly.

Attached files

If you attach a file to a logged chat, you are asked separately, for that upload, whether a copy may be stored. If you decline, only the file name, type and size are recorded and the file itself never leaves your browser.

Technical health information

The extension periodically reports whether it is functioning: how many records are waiting to upload, the last error, and whether it is signed in. This is used only to detect a broken installation.

3. What is never collected

  • Conversations you declined to log, including messages already sent in them.
  • Anything at all while recording is paused, or before you signed in and gave consent.
  • Activity on any website other than the four listed above.
  • Your browsing history, bookmarks, files, or activity in other applications.
  • Your password in readable form.

4. Automatic removal of personal details

Before any message text leaves your browser, email addresses and long digit sequences are automatically detected and replaced.

This cleaning cannot be applied to images or PDFs. A screenshot is stored exactly as it appears. Please decline any attachment containing an identity document, personal correspondence, or another person's work.

5. Why we collect it

Solely to conduct the academic research study you consented to join: analysing how students interact with AI assistants during learning, and reporting aggregate findings in academic publications. Individual participants are not identified in any publication.

6. How it is stored and protected

  • Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in a managed PostgreSQL database and object store operated by Supabase, hosted in [REGION].
  • Database access controls restrict every participant to their own records; one participant cannot read another's data.
  • Attached files are stored in a private bucket with no public URLs.
  • Access to the full dataset is limited to named members of the research team.

7. Who it is shared with

Data is not sold, rented, or transferred to any third party. It is not used for advertising, credit assessment, or any purpose unrelated to this research. It is accessible only to the named research team and to Supabase as our hosting provider, which processes it on our instructions.

Research outputs report aggregate or de-identified findings only.

Study data is retained for [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. five years from publication], in line with our institution's research data policy, and then deleted. Withdrawn participants' data is deleted on request as described below.
